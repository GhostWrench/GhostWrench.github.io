TTRPG-9000 Artificer Dice

The TTRPG-9000 Artificer Dice are battery powered electronic dice that utilizes user inputs to generate entropy (randomness). Great for DMs, GMs, power players, or anyone that needs to roll a lot of dice and doesn't want to carry a large bag everywhere. And unlike your cell phone rolling app, keeps you immersed in your game world. It is portable with a long battery life on 2 CR2302 button cells so you can keep your game area uncluttered and go anywhere with it. There are many ways to obtain your own!

  1. Build your own using our open source hardware and software
  2. Purchase a pre-made unit from the GhostWrench Etsy store
  3. Order a custom built unit by contacting us via phone or email
  4. Bulk orders for resale available as well at discounted prices!

There are many possible modifications available for custom units, contact us to inquire!

  1. Any color case that can be 3D printed
  2. Backlit screens for use in the dark
  3. 6 pin programming header for flashing custom software modifications
  4. Various software modifications (screen text, LED flashing, etc.)
  5. Alternate control knob designs
