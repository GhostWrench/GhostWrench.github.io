TTRPG-9000 Artificer Dice

Encumbered by dice at your RPG table? Hate breaking world immersion to roll on your phone? Looking for something unique for your dice collection? Then the TTRPG-9000 Artificer Dice handheld dice simulator is perfect for your adventure! The TTRPG-9000 features:

  1. A full set of RPG dice (d2, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100)
  2. Support for rolls of up to 80 dice
  3. A true random number generator with entropy derived from user inputs (just like real dice)
  4. Ultra efficient hadware with 100+ hours of use on 2 CR2032 cell batteries
  5. Open source PCB, case and software enable unlimited customization
  6. Compact, ergonomic and portable design
  7. 3D printed case with a large selection of colors
  8. Quality craftmanship with every unit being hand-made and tested in the USA
  9. Lead free PCB and solder
  10. Available wholesale orders and customization for your game shop, company or event!
